# Compliance Care Plan — Pricing Tiers

Three fixed-fee packages. Pick the tier by facility risk category (per
NEMA's project categorization) and monitoring frequency required by the
approval conditions — not by negotiation. Fixed fees are what make this
sellable fast: no re-scoping per client.

Fill in EEIS's actual day rates and lab-analysis costs before quoting —
placeholders below are structure, not final numbers.

## Tier 1 — Essential (low-risk sites)
**Best for:** small workshops, low-emission processors, sites with light
conditions of approval.

- 1 site visit per quarter (4/year)
- Visual waste-handling and storage audit each visit
- Permit/licence renewal tracking and reminder alerts
- 1-page compliance status report per visit
- Email support between visits

**Billing:** fixed quarterly fee, invoiced at the start of each quarter.

## Tier 2 — Standard (moderate-risk sites)
**Best for:** timber/wood processors, quarries, mid-size manufacturing —
EEIS's core existing client base.

Everything in Essential, plus:

- Effluent sampling and lab analysis each visit (where the site discharges)
- Air quality / dust monitoring each visit (where the site has stack or
  fugitive emissions)
- Waste-handling audit with corrective-action tracking
- Full compliance report mapped to the site's NEMA approval conditions
- Priority scheduling ahead of permit renewal deadlines
- 1 phone/email consultation per month included

**Billing:** fixed quarterly fee, invoiced at the start of each quarter.

## Tier 3 — Priority (higher-risk / larger sites)
**Best for:** larger contractors, multi-site operations, facilities under
closer NEMA scrutiny or with a history of non-compliance findings.

Everything in Standard, plus:

- Monthly site visit (not quarterly)
- Full effluent and air sampling every visit
- Annual self-monitoring/compliance audit report prepared for NEMA
  submission included in the compliance care plan (not billed separately)
- Direct support during any NEMA inspection or query
- Dedicated point of contact at EEIS

**Billing:** fixed monthly fee, invoiced at the start of each month.

## What's never "free-scope creep"

Anything outside the checklist in `service-scope-checklist.md` — a new
ESIA, an expansion project's fresh assessment, incident response, or a
NEMA enforcement action — is quoted and billed separately. The compliance care plan
covers routine monitoring, not new project work. State this explicitly in
the agreement so the fixed fee stays fixed.

## Upsell path

Track each client's renewal dates and sampling results in `app/index.html`.
A flagged upcoming renewal or a second facility coming online is the
natural moment to propose either a tier upgrade or a second compliance care plan.
