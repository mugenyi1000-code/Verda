*This is a starting template to get a signature quickly. Have it reviewed
by counsel before use, and adapt it to the specific engagement.*

**Between:** EEIS ("the Consultant")
**And:** [Client legal name] ("the Client")
**Site(s) covered:** [site name / location]
**Effective date:** [date]
**Initial term:** [12 months, auto-renewing unless cancelled with 30 days'
notice]

## 1. Services
The Consultant will provide the compliance care plan services
described in Tier [1 / 2 / 3] of EEIS's Compliance Care Plan Pricing
Schedule, dated [date], attached as Annex A. In summary: [X] site visit(s)
per [quarter/month] including waste-handling audit, [effluent sampling /
air monitoring, if applicable], and permit/licence renewal tracking, with a
compliance status report delivered after each visit.

## 2. Fees
- Fee: [amount] per [quarter/month], invoiced at the start of each period.
- Payment terms: due within [14/30] days of invoice.
- The fee is fixed for the services in Annex A. Work outside that scope
  (see Section 6 of the Service Scope Checklist) will be quoted and agreed
  separately before it proceeds.

## 3. Term and termination
Either party may terminate with [30/60] days' written notice. The Client
remains responsible for fees for any period already invoiced or for
services already delivered.

## 4. Client obligations
The Client will provide site access, a site contact, and relevant permit
documentation, and will implement corrective actions identified in the
Consultant's reports within [30] days or an agreed remediation timeline.

## 5. Reporting and use of results
Reports are prepared for the Client's internal compliance management and
for submission to NEMA or other regulators as required. The Consultant is
not responsible for the Client's actions or inactions on report findings.

## 6. Confidentiality
Both parties will keep the other's confidential business information
confidential, except where disclosure is required by law or regulation
(including mandatory NEMA reporting).
